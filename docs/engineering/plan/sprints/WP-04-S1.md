### Rules for every lane

- Clean-room. No upstream code; WP-04 needs no upstream lab.
- Lab root `/Volumes/BackCat/rivqen-p2-lab` (DL-138). Do not use `/Volumes/BackCat/rivqen-lab`.
- Never touch `emulator-5554` or a simulator of the human. Give every `adb` call the lab serial `emulator-5602`. Run one Android emulator at a time.
- Keep at least 20 GB free on the system volume. Put the Rust `target/`, Gradle and DerivedData in the lab root, not in `/tmp`.
- Lanes do not start mutation runs. The architect gives each mutation run a slot. `cargo-mutants -j 1 --timeout 60`, `RUST_TEST_THREADS=4`, `--iterate`. Stryker only detached with `nohup`; then check `git status`.
- No secrets and no personal data in traces or recordings. Cookie values are synthetic. No trust-all TLS, no ATS exception, no private API.
- Platform facts come from the documentation with a source, or are NOT FOUND.
