## 1. Kickoff

Kickoff on 2026-11-09. WP-02, the only dependency of WP-05, is merged (2026-10-31, #5). WP-04 is merged into `main` (bd75612, #9, 2026-11-09): gate G2 is passed (H-137) and ADR-002 is accepted (H-135: UniFFI control plane and a C ABI byte channel, at most one copy per byte per direction). The first commit of `wp/WP-05` marks WP-04 done and removes the "in force when merged" notes.

P3 builds the Rust core. WP-05 is its base; WP-06 (template), WP-07 (cache), WP-08 (session and diff), WP-09 (streaming and FFI) and WP-23 (legacy modules) build on it. Gate G3: "Core passes unit, property, fuzz and golden tests; `unsafe` reviewed; resource limits enforced".
