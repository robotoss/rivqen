### Decisions of the human for S2

| ID | Question | Decision (2026-11-22) |
|---|---|---|
| H-150 | Q-27: the consent of the server for authenticated caching of an RQP response | **An explicit `Cache-Control: private` or `public`.** A credentialed RQP response is stored only when the app sets `authenticated_cache` and the response has one of the two directives. Legacy mode keeps CL-25 (`cache-offline` is the consent). Not chosen: no `no-store` is enough; a new manifest member |
| H-151 | ADR-011: which entries the driver encrypts | **Every entry**: scoped entries with the key of their partition, `public` entries with the key of `public`. Not chosen: scoped and marked entries only; authenticated partitions only |

### Decisions of the architect

| ID | Topic | Decision |
|---|---|---|
