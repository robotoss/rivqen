### Human decisions of the kickoff

| ID | Question | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| H-156 | The URL parser of the product (the canonical URL of the cache key, the URL U of the binding; DL-187, DL-060) | **The crate `url` =2.5.9 with `idna_adapter` =1.0.0 as a direct pin.** A host that is not ASCII is rejected: no session, the WebView loads normally. Not chosen: `url` with ICU4X (33 crates, +182 KB); an own parser (no dependency, one more senior lane, a fuzz target and a comparison with the web-platform-tests data) |
| H-157 | Q-26: the freshness and the offline policy of a patched RQP snapshot | **Option (c).** The patched snapshot keeps the response directives of the base snapshot S and takes the time of the delta as its response time, as a `304` does (RFC 9111 §4.3.4). Not chosen: (a) no lifetime; (b) the times of S |
| H-158 | Q-25: the size rule of the full RQP document F | **A client limit for F.** The client reads F up to M-01 plus the largest manifest element; the original document O keeps M-01. Servers and fixtures do not change. Not chosen: the server sends no manifest when F is over 5 MiB; no change |
