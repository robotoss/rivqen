## 2. Plan

Two phases. Phase A studies and proposes (no rule change). The human decides on the evidence (new rows H-71 and later in this record). Phase B implements the decided rules. Phase B briefs are final when the decisions exist; the architect then fills in the `rules:` member of each brief.

### Contracts (architect, before lanes start)

- Corpus data: the architect fetches the corpus once into `<session scratchpad>/corpus-data/` with `tools/rqp-markup/corpus/fetch.mjs` and `layout.mjs` (the S1 URL list). Both study lanes read it. No page bytes go into the repository.
- Study report format (both lanes): `evidence/markup-<topic>/report.md` with sections 1 Question, 2 Method, 3 Results (tables with numbers), 4 Security analysis, 5 Proposed rule text (as a diff of `markup.md`, not applied), 6 Options for the human (recommended first, with cost), 7 Facts and sources (FACT with date, or NOT FOUND); machine results in `evidence/markup-<topic>/*.json`.
- Phase B: the rule text in `markup.md` and the golden fixtures of T-03 are the contract for T-04…T-08.
