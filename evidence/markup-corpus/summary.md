## 12. Limits

- The corpus is a hand-made list of popular sites and inner pages found on their home pages, not a random sample. Large sites and English and Russian pages have more weight. Sites that block test clients (HTTP 403, bot walls) are missing.
- The pages are the HTML that the server sent to a client without JavaScript and without cookies. A WebView page after scripts can differ; an RQP template is the server HTML, so this is the right input.
- "Alone" assumes that the removal of one class leaves the other rules unchanged. The classifier continues after a violation with the token-stack behavior of M-05.
- The simulated blocks are a heuristic for where a template author puts blocks (DESIGN). A real template would put blocks on smaller, controlled regions.
- The rule probe and the classifier show which rules reject; they do not show that a relaxation is safe. Relaxing a rule is a human decision after a parse5 study.
